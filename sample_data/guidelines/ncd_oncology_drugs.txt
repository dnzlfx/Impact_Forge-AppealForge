NCD 110.6 - Off-Label Use of Antineoplastic Drugs

Indications and Limitations of Coverage. The Medicare program covers the off-label use of Food and Drug Administration (FDA)-approved antineoplastic drugs when the use is for a medically accepted indication. A medically accepted indication is defined by one of the following:

1. The use is included in one of the standard compendia (e.g., NCCN Drugs & Biologics Compendium, AHFS Drug Information, DrugDex).
2. The use is supported by published peer-reviewed clinical evidence demonstrating safety and effectiveness for the specific indication.
3. The use is included in clinical practice guidelines developed by nationally recognized professional organizations.

Conditions of Coverage. All of the following conditions must be met:

1. The drug is FDA-approved, though not necessarily for the specific indication being treated.
2. The drug is used for the treatment of cancer.
3. The off-label use is for a medically accepted indication as defined above.

Documentation Requirements. The medical record must document the specific cancer diagnosis, the drug regimen, the evidence or compendium supporting the off-label indication, and the clinical rationale for the choice of therapy. Coverage decisions for off-label uses are made on a case-by-case basis.

Non-covered Indications. Off-label uses of drugs that are not supported by compendia or peer-reviewed evidence, and uses for non-cancer conditions, are not covered under this NCD. Compounded drugs that do not meet FDA quality standards are not covered.
