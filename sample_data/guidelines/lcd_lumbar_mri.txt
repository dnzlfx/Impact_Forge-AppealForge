LCD L34212 - Magnetic Resonance Imaging of the Lumbar Spine

Limitations of Coverage. This LCD applies to MRI of the lumbar spine performed without contrast. Coverage is provided when the medical record documents one or more of the following indications:

1. Radiculopathy with positive straight leg raise (Lasègue sign) or reflex changes refractory to at least 4 weeks of first-line analgesics and conservative therapy.
2. Suspected spinal stenosis with neurogenic claudication (bilateral leg pain with ambulation, relieved by flexion) in patients over 60 years of age.
3. Progressive or severe neurologic deficit, cauda equina syndrome, or suspected neoplasm or infection requiring urgent imaging.
4. Pre-operative planning for lumbar decompression or fusion surgery when the clinical diagnosis is established and surgical intervention is contemplated.

Documentation Requirements. The clinical record must document the history and physical examination findings supporting the indication for the examination. Standard x-rays or other plain films do not provide sufficient information to evaluate the spinal canal contents and are not a substitute.

CPT/HCPCS Codes. This LCD applies to CPT 72148 (MRI of lumbar spinal canal and contents, without contrast material). The documentation must reflect the specific medical necessity for this code.
